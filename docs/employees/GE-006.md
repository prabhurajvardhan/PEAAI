## Implementation Summary

The M06 Transition Engine has been fully implemented with:

1. **Face-to-Story Transition**: Dissolves the companion face into story scenes with configurable patterns
2. **Story-to-Face Transition**: Merges story scenes back into the companion face
3. **Pixel Morphing**: Smooth pixel transformations with 6 preset effects
4. **Dissolve Effects**: 6 dissolve patterns (grid, particle, noise, radial, spiral, wave)
5. **Transition Timeline**: Complete timing coordination with hooks and interrupt handling
6. **Main Orchestrator**: `TransitionEngine` class that ties all components together
